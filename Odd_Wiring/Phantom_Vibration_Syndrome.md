## Short #1 — Psychology pillar

**Topic:** Phantom Vibration Syndrome (the "my phone buzzed but it didn't" feeling)

**Script (≈34 sec, calm/authoritative pacing):**

| Time | Line - voiceover | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:02 | "Your phone just buzzed. Except... it didn't." | *(bold, centered)* "IT DIDN'T BUZZ." |
| 0:02–0:08 | "This has an actual name — Phantom Vibration Syndrome. It was first named by a researcher in 2003, and some studies put it at nine out of ten phone users." | "PHANTOM VIBRATION SYNDROME" (big callout) |
| 0:08–0:16 | "Here's the wiring behind it: your brain is drowning in sensory input all day, so it predicts what it's about to feel before it fully feels it. Expecting a buzz, it sometimes mistakes a muscle twitch or fabric brushing your leg for the real thing." | subtle brain/nerve motion graphic |
| 0:16–0:26 | "One study tracked medical interns starting a high-stress job. At the start, 78% had felt phantom vibrations. By the most stressful month — 96%. Once the stress dropped, the phantom buzzing dropped with it." | "78% → 96%" stat callout |
| 0:26–0:32 | “So if you’ve been feeling your phone buzz more than usual lately — here’s the trick: next time it happens, don’t reach for it. Just notice the urge and let it pass.” | *(slow fade text)* "IT'S NOT YOUR PHONE." |
| 0:32–0:36 | “You’re not reacting to your phone anymore. You’re reacting to your own nervous system.” | "👇 COMMENT" |

**B-roll/visual shot list:**

- Close-up: phone resting on a table/in a pocket, slight ambient motion
- Hand instinctively reaching to check a phone
- Abstract neural/synapse motion graphic (stock or simple animation) during the mechanism explanation
- Busy office/street montage (quick cut) for "drowning in sensory input"
- Clean number/stat card animation for the 78%→96% beat

**Title:** The Real Reason You Feel Your Phone Buzz (When It Didn't)

**Description:** Phantom Vibration Syndrome is real, named, and studied — and stress controls how often you feel it. #Shorts #OddWiring #Psychology #DidYouKnow #Brain
